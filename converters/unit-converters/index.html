💡 Metric, imperial and everything between

Whether your reference point is metric or imperial, these converters bridge the gap instantly. Cooks can switch grams to cups and Celsius to Fahrenheit; travellers can turn kilometres into miles and kilograms into pounds; students and engineers can move between pascals, bars and PSI, or joules, calories and kilowatt-hours. Each converter accepts a value in one unit and shows the equivalent in the other using exact, internationally-defined conversion factors, so you never have to remember awkward multipliers or risk a rounding mistake in your head.

🧮 How unit conversion works

Most conversions are a single multiplication or division by a fixed factor — for example, 1 inch is exactly 2.54 centimetres, and 1 kilogram is about 2.2046 pounds. Temperature is the notable exception, because scales like Celsius and Fahrenheit have different zero points, so the conversion uses a formula rather than a simple factor. Our converters handle all of this for you and let you control the rounding precision, which matters when you need either a quick estimate or a value accurate to several decimal places for technical work. Seeing the underlying relationship also helps you sanity-check results and build intuition for the units you use most.
