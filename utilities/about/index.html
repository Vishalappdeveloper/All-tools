Who we are

allfreecalculators.in is an independent project dedicated to building free, easy-to-use online calculators and unit converters. What began as a handful of everyday tools has grown into a library of more than 2,979 calculators across 46 categories, used by students, professionals, tradespeople and curious minds around the world.

Our mission

We believe that quick, reliable calculations should be available to everyone, for free, on any device. Our goal is to remove friction: no downloads, no sign-ups and no clutter — just the answer you need, instantly, along with a clear explanation of how it was reached.

What we offer

  • 2,979+ tools spanning finance, math, health, dates, science, conversions and more.
  • Clear explanations — every calculator includes a how-to guide, formula, examples and FAQs.
  • Privacy by design — all calculations run in your browser; your data stays with you.
  • Mobile-first, fast-loading pages that meet modern Core Web Vitals standards.

Why trust allfreecalculators.in

Each tool is based on standard, widely-accepted formulas and is reviewed for accuracy. We are transparent about how our calculators work and we encourage users to verify important financial, medical or legal figures with a qualified professional. We are committed to keeping allfreecalculators.in free, useful and respectful of your privacy.

Get in touch
